Privacy Policy & Terms

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

1. 100% On-Device Privacy Guaranteed

All daily scroll tracking metrics, custom quiet hours, streak information, and check-in mood logs are exclusively written and stored inside your local memory databases (`UserDefaults` on iOS and `localStorage` on browsers). No data is transmitted to third-party databases, tracking tools, or cloud networks.

2. Screen Time FamilyControls Integration

On iOS, the application uses Apple's native Screen Time API permissions (`NSFamilyControlsUsageDescription`) to apply block shields on designated targets. The App cannot read, parse, or capture messages, photos, or keystrokes inside managed apps.
