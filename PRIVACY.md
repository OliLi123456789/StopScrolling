# Privacy Policy for Pause

*Effective Date: August 5, 2026*

Pause is a supportive companion application designed to help you practice mindful scrolling and gain awareness of screen-time habits.

## 1. 100% Privacy-First & Local Storage
Pause values your privacy above everything else.
- **No Remote Servers:** All configuration settings, daily scroll metrics, streak statistics, and after-scroll reflection responses are saved exclusively on your local device (using `UserDefaults` on iOS and `localStorage` on the Web Companion).
- **No Tracking or Third-Party Analytics:** We do not track your activity, collect anonymized usage details, or integrate third-party data tracking SDKs.
- **No Account Registration:** Pause operates anonymously. No email, password, or third-party social logins (like Facebook or Google) are requested or stored.

## 2. iOS Screen Time API (FamilyControls)
On iOS, Pause utilizes Apple's native Screen Time APIs (`FamilyControls`, `ManagedSettings`) to manage gentle intervention overlays before opening selected apps.
- All selection parameters and tokens are encrypted locally on your device.
- Pause cannot see, inspect, transmit, or parse the content, keys, or keystrokes inside any managed application.

## 3. Immediate Account & Data Deletion
Since all data is saved locally, you can wipe and delete your entire configuration, history logs, and subscription states instantly:
- Simply tap **"Delete All My Data & Account"** inside the Settings screen to wipe your storage clean.

## 4. Updates
We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date.
